Research

May 2026

Working paper · SSRN

Indonesia: Energy & Resources Policy

Sole author Political Economy of Energy and Resources Policy

How Indonesia went from early OPEC member to net oil importer, and how it is responding: a pivot to natural gas and LNG, the shift from Production Sharing Contracts to Gross Split and Flexible Choice models, Pertamina’s 2021 takeover of the Rokan Block, and projects like Tangguh LNG Train 3 and the Abadi Field. It concludes that energy security depends on regulatory dynamism and new deepwater exploration.

Aug – Dec 2025

Graduate paper · Rice Economics

The Dynamic Impact of Feed-in-Tariffs on Solar Capacity: Evidence from the Chinese Border

Co-author With Kanwal Deep Singh and Ty Hampton, under Dr. Tilsa G. Ore Monago, Rice University Department of Economics

Uses a dynamic spatial regression discontinuity design on 426 county-year observations to show that China’s 2013 Feed-in-Tariff added 68.35 MW of solar capacity in the subsidized zone by 2016, after a three-year implementation lag, and that the subsidy outweighed the zone’s lower solar radiation.

Working paper · SSRN

Asia’s Renewable Energy Future: A Quantitative Analysis of Development

Primary author With Dr. Pablo Troncoso, faculty advisor and co-author

A study of energy production, use, and demand across nine Asia-Pacific economies using World Bank and IEA data, with a proposed Renewable Index that ranks where further renewable adoption would do the most good, and a comparison of national strategies in Japan, South Korea, and India.
